How It Works

Our tool uses the powerful Tesseract OCR engine, optimized for the web. When you upload an image, our algorithm scans every pixel to identify character shapes and patterns, converting them into digital text instantly.

Whether it's a scanned document, a screenshot of a webpage, or a photo of a handwritten note, Jpgtotext.pro handles it with precision.
